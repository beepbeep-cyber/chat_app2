# Privacy Policy for Chat App 2

**Last updated: January 18, 2025**

## Introduction

Chat App 2 ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- Email address (for authentication)
- Display name and profile photo
- User ID (automatically generated)

### 2. Chat Data
- Messages (text, images, voice, video)
- Chat history
- Group memberships
- Call logs (duration, participants)

### 3. Automatically Collected Data
- Device information (OS version, device model)
- IP address
- App usage statistics

## How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
- Provide chat and calling services
- Authenticate your account
- Improve app performance
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage

- All data is stored securely using **Firebase Cloud Firestore**
- Messages are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- We do NOT sell your data to third parties

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

### Firebase (Google)
- Authentication
- Cloud Firestore (database)
- Cloud Storage (media files)
- Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Agora.io
- Video/Audio calling
- Privacy Policy: https://www.agora.io/en/privacy-policy/

### Google Sign-In
- OAuth authentication
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Opt-out of data collection

## Data Retention

- Active accounts: Data retained indefinitely
- Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
